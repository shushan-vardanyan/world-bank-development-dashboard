A Power BI dashboard comparing Armenia with Azerbaijan, Georgia, and Türkiye across economic, health, digital access, and labor market indicators.

## Indicators

- GDP per capita (current US$)
- Life expectancy at birth (years)
- Individuals using the Internet (% of population)
- Unemployment (% of total labor force)

## Dashboard features

- Country and year filters
- Country comparisons for the selected year
- Development trends from 2020 to 2024
- GDP per capita and life expectancy comparison

## Data

The Excel workbook contains annual World Bank data for 15 countries from 2010 to 2024. The dashboard focuses on Armenia and selected regional peers from 2020 to 2024. Missing source values are kept blank.

Source links and indicator definitions are included in the `About_and_Sources` worksheet.
